Who we are

One operator for your entire cold chain

FROZEN CARGO TRANSPORT S.A.S. is a company specialized in end-to-end logistics services, focused on temperature-controlled storage, cargo custody, inventory management, transport and complementary operations.

Our highly trained team, standardized processes and monitoring technology guarantee a reliable, secure service tailored to each client's specific needs, from receiving the product to its final delivery.

  • Weekly traceability under Good Storage Practices (GSP)
  • A quality engineer in charge of process verification
  • Certified forklift-handling personnel

What we do

Three pillars, one unbroken cold chain

From the first receipt at the dock to the last mile, we cover every link in your operation with traceable processes and specialized staff.

01

Temperature-controlled storage

Chiller rooms (0°C to 4°C) and freezer rooms (-18°C) designed to preserve temperature-sensitive products, with inventory custody and management under constant control.

02

Transport & distribution

Local, regional and national coverage with real-time traceability, including direct transfers to and from the ports of Barranquilla and Cartagena.

  • Consolidated transport
  • Last-mile delivery
  • Nationwide palletized cargo
  • Port transfers — Barranquilla / Cartagena
03

Inventory management & traceability

Physical and documentary verification of every movement, with inbound, outbound and balance reports handled under strict confidentiality.

  • Physical & documentary verification
  • Inventory system logging
  • Movement reporting
  • Joint monthly physical inventories
