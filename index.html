Who we are

One operator for your entire cold chain

FROZEN CARGO TRANSPORT S.A.S. is a company specialized in end-to-end logistics services, focused on temperature-controlled storage, cargo custody, inventory management, transport and complementary operations.

Our highly trained team, standardized processes and monitoring technology guarantee a reliable, secure service tailored to each client's specific needs, from receiving the product to its final delivery.

  • Weekly traceability under Good Storage Practices (GSP)
  • A quality engineer in charge of process verification
  • Certified forklift-handling personnel

Where we operate

Coverage

Our cold chain operations center is strategically located in Barranquilla, the logistics heart of the Caribbean Coast. From there we directly manage and coordinate distribution across the region and nationwide, leveraging our geographic position to offer fast response times and optimized costs.

We also have a specialized fleet monitored in real time that lets us provide temperature-controlled freight transport nationwide.
